# Medical AI Operations Dashboard

## 🏥 Project Overview
This project is an advanced, AI-powered administration and operations dashboard tailored for **Ambulatory Surgery Centers (ASCs)** or hospital surgical departments. Its primary goal is to optimize operating room (OR) efficiency, track surgeon performance, manage costs (supplies, implants, labor), and maximize overall profitability. 

A standout feature is the integration of an AI Operations Analyst that processes operational data to provide actionable, cost-saving recommendations.

---

## ⚙️ Core Workflows & Features

### 1. Command Center & Business Analysis
*   **Executive Dashboard:** Provides a high-level view of facility-wide metrics, including OR utilization rates, month-to-date (MTD) margins, and revenue per hour.
*   **Profitability Tracking:** Breaks down case profitability into tiers (Highly Profitable to Unprofitable) and tracks contribution margins across different facility locations (e.g., Main ASC, South ASC).

### 2. Surgeon Performance Profiling
*   **Detailed Scorecards:** Tracks individual surgeon metrics such as cases completed, average procedure duration, room turnover times, and on-time start rates.
*   **Financial & CPT Breakdown:** Analyzes the net margin generated by specific CPT codes (e.g., Knee Scope, Cataract Surgery) for each surgeon.
*   **Payer Mix:** Visualizes the revenue distribution across Commercial, Medicare, and Self-Pay patients.

### 3. Schedule & OR Block Optimization
*   **OR Block Scheduling:** Manages the daily timeline of operating rooms, tracking scheduled surgeries against system blocks and maintenance downtime.
*   **Waitlist Management:** Queues pending cases with their expected durations and projected margins to fill sudden cancellations.

### 4. Supply Chain & Cost Management
*   **Preference Card Auditing:** Compares a surgeon's actual supply usage against standard baseline preference cards to calculate supply variance and identify overruns.
*   **Waste Tracking:** Logs opened vs. used items (implants, sutures, biologics) to quantify the exact dollar amount of surgical waste per case.

### 5. AI-Driven Insights (The "AI Analyst")
*   **Automated Recommendations:** The system analyzes inefficiencies (e.g., "Average supply card cost is 31.2% higher than standard") and provides concrete AI insights (e.g., "Consolidating spine rod suppliers can retrieve $6,500 in monthly margin leakage").
*   **Interactive Chatbot:** Allows administrators to query the system for operational advice and data deep-dives.

## 🛠️ Technology Stack

### Frontend Architecture
*   **React 19:** The core UI library used for building modular components.
*   **Vite:** A blazing-fast frontend build tool and development server, replacing Create React App for better performance.
*   **Recharts:** A composable charting library used heavily to render the Line, Bar, and Pie charts for financial trends and payer mix visualizations.
*   **Lucide React:** Provides clean, modern SVG icons for the UI.
*   **React DatePicker:** Handles calendar and date selection inputs for scheduling.

### Backend & Database
*   **Supabase (`@supabase/supabase-js`):** An open-source Firebase alternative providing the PostgreSQL database, authentication, and real-time subscriptions. Custom SQL scripts are used for database setup.

### Artificial Intelligence
*   **Google Generative AI (`@google/generative-ai`):** Powers the `AIOperationsAnalyst` and `Chatbot` components. It processes operational JSON data to generate dynamic, context-aware insights.

### Utilities & Export
*   **jsPDF:** Enables the generation and downloading of PDF reports directly from the dashboard.
*   **XLSX (SheetJS):** Used for parsing or exporting Excel spreadsheets.
*   **SweetAlert2:** Used for beautiful, responsive popup alerts and modal dialogs.
